fix log / email deliverability

GHL emails were landing in spam. Now they reach the inbox.

Emails sent from a GoHighLevel account kept going to spam. I adjusted the GHL email sending settings and the domain's DNS records until SPF, DKIM and DMARC all passed, so mail servers could verify the emails really came from that domain. After that, they started arriving in the inbox.
